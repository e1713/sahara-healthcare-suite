The Voice Intake & EHR Integration Engine (Module 2) automates clinical note-taking during doctor consultations. It transcribes the live code-switched conversation, auto-fills a medical chart, and builds a structured SOAP draft with FHIR / EHR JSON output. Every field stays a draft until a physician verifies it.

## Record a consultation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start recording">
    Select **Start Consultation Recording**, or **Load Case 1 of 5 Gold Standard Cases** for a scripted example.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tag each speaker">
    Under **Recording Speaker**, select **Doctor** or **Patient** before each segment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the chart fill in">
    The **Auto-Filled EHR Medical Chart** updates as fields are recognized: chief complaint, duration, BP, pulse, temperature, and prescribed medications.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the SOAP draft">
    Select **Generate SOAP & ICD-10**. Edit any field with **Edit SOAP Draft**. Fields you change manually are not overwritten by streaming updates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify and sign off">
    Review the draft, then select **Verify & Sign Off Record**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export or commit">
    Use **Preview FHIR JSON**, **Export FHIR JSON**, **Summary PDF/Text**, or **Commit to EHR**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The SOAP draft

- **S - Subjective:** symptoms reported by the patient.
- **O - Objective:** observations and vitals.
- **A - Assessment:** ICD-10 code suggestion.
- **P - Plan & Prescription:** treatment and stewardship notes.

<Note>
  The backend validates structured SOAP JSON. Free prose returns a manual-review draft and does not generate ICD-10 codes. Prose-to-SOAP/ICD-10 generation is not complete.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Physician verification is required before any FHIR EMR sync. Medication safety is not enforced end to end, so verify medication details independently.
</Warning>
