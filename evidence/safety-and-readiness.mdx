AfriHealth AI is built to make clinical speech easier to document, never harder to verify. Every transcript and generated artifact is a draft for a qualified clinician. This page explains the safety boundaries of the prototype and the gates that remain open before operational use.

## Human-in-the-loop by design

- Transcripts, triage summaries, SOAP drafts, and ICD-10 suggestions are drafts until a clinician verifies them.
- Module 2 requires **Verify & Sign Off** before any EHR commit.
- Triage and follow-up support decisions. They do **not** diagnose, contact patients autonomously, or dispatch care.

## Open readiness gates

<Warning>
  Do not use the prototype with identifiable patient data until these gates are closed.
</Warning>

- Transcript retention and deletion controls.
- Built-in clinician authentication and role-based access.
- Verified production gateway configuration and exact production CORS origins.
- End-to-end medication safety enforcement. Until then, verify all medication details independently.

## Delivery priorities

1. **Close production safety gates:** retention/deletion, authentication, and gateway checks.
2. **Complete clinician-reviewed workflows:** validate transcript-to-SOAP and coding, and add a backend medication safety gate.
3. **Strengthen evaluation evidence:** expand consented, de-identified Amharic-English evaluation.
4. **Verify release and handoff:** confirm deployment and complete the team handoff checklist.
