The Frontline Community Triage Agent (Module 1) supports community health workers (CHWs) with voice-driven triage documentation. It runs with noise suppression active and in **Amharic-English Code-Switch** mode, so workers can speak naturally across both languages.

## Gold Standard cases

Five pre-loaded cases let you try the module without recording live audio:

| Case | Scenario |
| --- | --- |
| Case 1 | Acute Emergency (Chest Pain & Fever) |
| Case 2 | Maternal Post-Op Follow-up |
| Case 3 | Pediatric Respiratory & High Fever |
| Case 4 | Acute Gastroenteritis & Dehydration |
| Case 5 | Chronic Hypertension & Diabetes Follow-up |

## Record a triage conversation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your input">
    Select a Gold Standard case, or tap the microphone to record live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the recording">
    Before upload, use **Review saved recording before upload** to listen back. Select **Download local copy** to keep a copy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload to the speech engine">
    Select **Save and upload recording to Intron**. Audio is processed by the Intron Sahara v2.5 ASR engine.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the results">
    Review the transcript and its **Confidence** score, the **Extracted Medical Entities**, and the **Triage Classification**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the recommendation">
    Select **Speak recommendation aloud** to have the referral recommendation read out.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The triage classification and referral recommendation are decision support. A qualified clinician must verify them. The module does not diagnose or dispatch care.
</Warning>
