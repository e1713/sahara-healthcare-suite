AfriHealth AI is built by **ETH-1713-AI**, a company focused on making clinical speech easier to document for multilingual care teams in Africa. Our first product turns Amharic-English code-switched conversations into draft clinical records that clinicians review and approve.

## Our mission

Make clinical speech easier to document, never harder to verify. Every output AfriHealth AI produces is a draft for a qualified clinician, so care teams save time without giving up control.
