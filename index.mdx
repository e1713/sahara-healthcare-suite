AfriHealth AI Unified Care Suite helps multilingual care teams document clinical conversations by voice. It captures Amharic-English code-switched speech, transcribes it with the Intron Sahara v2.5 speech engine, and produces draft triage summaries, SOAP notes, and follow-up escalations that a qualified clinician always reviews before use.

<Warning>
  AfriHealth AI is a functional prototype. It is **not ready for identifiable patient data**. See [Safety and Readiness](/evidence/safety-and-readiness) before any operational use.
</Warning>

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the prototype">
    Go to [sahara-healthcare-suite.pages.dev](https://sahara-healthcare-suite.pages.dev/) in a modern browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run system diagnostics">
    Confirm your microphone, speech engine, and voice output work. See the [Quickstart](/quickstart).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try a Gold Standard case">
    Load one of the five pre-recorded cases to see each module in action without a microphone.
  </Step>
</Steps>
