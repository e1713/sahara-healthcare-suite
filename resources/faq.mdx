These answers cover the questions new AfriHealth AI users ask most often, from microphone setup to what the prototype can safely be used for.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My microphone does not work">
    Open the diagnostics panel and confirm **Browser Secure Context** passes. Browsers only allow microphone capture over HTTPS or localhost. Then select **Test Mic** and allow access in your browser's site permissions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I try the modules without a microphone?">
    Yes. Use **Pre-load Audio** or **Judge Mode (No Mic Req.)** with any of the five Gold Standard cases.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The Intron Sahara API check fails">
    Select **Ping API** again. If it still fails, the backend Intron proxy is unreachable. Contact your AfriHealth AI administrator.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use real patient data?">
    No. The prototype is not ready for identifiable patient data. See [Safety and Readiness](/evidence/safety-and-readiness).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why did my SOAP draft not include ICD-10 codes?">
    ICD-10 codes are only generated from structured SOAP JSON. Free prose returns a manual-review draft without codes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does AfriHealth AI make clinical decisions?">
    No. All outputs are drafts and decision support. A qualified clinician must review and sign off.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which languages are supported?">
    Amharic-English today. Afaan Oromoo-English and Tigrinya-English are on the roadmap. See [Languages](/resources/languages).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
